Some plants grow in places where they do not belong. They can spread very fast.

These are called invasive plants when they harm the local environment.

They can take space, water, or light from native plants that belong there.

Meet the plant helpers!

🐐 Goats eat many kinds of leaves, weeds, and shrubs.

🌼 When trained workers guide goats to the right area, goats may help remove unwanted plants so native plants have more room to grow.

🐐 How can goats help the land?

🔬 Science Reality Check

Goats can be helpful—but they are not magic! Click each question to reveal the facts.

🐐 Do goats eat only harmful plants?

No. Goats may also eat native plants. Workers must choose the right place and watch the goats closely.

🌿 Will goats remove every invasive plant forever?

No. Some plants grow back from seeds or roots. The site may need more care after goats leave.

🔥 Can grazing prevent every wildfire?

No. Goats may reduce some dry plants that can burn. They cannot stop every fire, and grazing must be carefully planned.

🌎 Are goats always the best tool?

No. Goats can help in some places. Workers may also need hand tools, other safe methods, and native planting.

Remember: Goats are one land-care tool. People need a good plan.
